About StudyVault

StudyVault is a free platform built by students, for students across India. The goal is simple — make quality study material like previous year papers, notes, and guides accessible to every student regardless of their college or background.

Whether you are preparing for exams, trying to understand patterns, or just want a head start — StudyVault can help you study smarter, boost your performance, and save hours of searching.

Privacy Policy

Data We Collect
We collect only the information necessary to provide our service — such as uploaded file metadata (title, course, year) and basic usage analytics.

How We Use It
Your data is used solely to display and serve study materials. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

Uploaded Content
Files you upload are stored securely on Cloudinary. By uploading, you confirm you have the right to share the content.

Cookies
We use minimal cookies for basic site functionality only. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

Contact
For privacy concerns, email us at studyvaultapp@gmail.com

DMCA & Content Policy

StudyVault respects intellectual property rights. If you believe any content on our platform infringes your copyright, please contact us at studyvaultapp@gmail.com.

Include: your name, the infringing URL, and proof of ownership.
